Scope of Work

Full gut, new finishes throughout.

$51,079.70

The existing vanity, tub, and floor tile come out, along with the built-in makeup counter nook, the medicine cabinets, the archway connecting the shower to the closet hallway, the soffit above the shower and the soffit above the vanity (surface-level demo only — utilities behind the soffits are not being relocated), and the wall column currently separating the shower area, opening it up for a full glass surround (demo and drywall repair only, not structural work). Drywall is repaired throughout wherever those were removed. The tub is set and connected, the double vanity's two sinks are roughed in and finished, the toilet is reset, and the shower valve/trim are reset within the existing shower footprint. Recessed lighting goes in throughout the room, with electrical roughed in for three LED mirrors, a dedicated outlet for a bidet toilet, and a replaced vent fan. New tile is installed on all floors, and the shower is tiled floor-to-ceiling on two walls — the third side is a wraparound glass enclosure instead of tile, with blocking and a dam built in, a new bench built into the shower, and a niche added to the shower wall. A custom vanity is built with shaker-style white oak doors per a direct quote from our carpenter, with the stain/finish work completed as a separate step. The former makeup nook is demoed and left prepped — the storage closet planned for that space is not yet included; see the note below. Open shelving is also added on both sides where the medicine cabinets came out, and near the vent fan area. The vanity countertop is fabricated and installed, new baseboard goes in throughout, the three LED mirrors and your hardware (towel racks, TP holders) are installed, and the entire room is painted.

Estimated Project Timeline

Four weeks, stage by stage.

A general outline of how this project is expected to move. Actual pacing can shift depending on material lead times (especially the shower glass and vanity) and what's found once demo starts.

WeekStage
Week 1
Demo & Rough-In
Full demo — vanity, tub, floor tile, medicine cabinets, makeup nook, archway, both soffits, and the wall column. Drywall repair throughout. Rough plumbing and rough electrical, including the bidet outlet and vent fan.
Week 2
Shower Build & Tile
Shower blocking, dam, bench, and niche framed and built. Hot mop and waterproofing. Floor tile and shower tile installed throughout.
Week 3
Carpentry & Fixtures
Custom vanity and open shelving installed. Countertop fabricated and installed. Tub and toilet set. Shower glass enclosure installed. Finish plumbing.
Week 4
Finish & Trim-Out
Baseboard, mirrors, and hardware installed. Full room paint. Punch list and final walkthrough.

This is a general estimate, not a guaranteed schedule — timing can move based on material availability and what we find once the space is opened up.
