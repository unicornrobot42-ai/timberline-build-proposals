Scope of Work

Full gut, new finishes throughout.

$49,014.90

The existing vanity, tub, and floor tile come out, along with the built-in makeup counter nook, the archway connecting the shower to the closet hallway, and the soffit above the shower (surface-level demo only — utilities behind the soffit are not being relocated). Drywall is repaired throughout wherever those were removed. The tub is set and connected, the double vanity's two sinks are roughed in and finished, the toilet is reset, and the shower valve/trim are reset within the existing shower footprint. Recessed lighting goes in throughout the room, with electrical roughed in for three LED mirrors and a new light inside the pantry closet. New tile is installed on all floors, and the shower is tiled floor-to-ceiling on two walls — the third side is a wraparound glass enclosure instead of tile, with blocking and a dam built in as part of the shower. A custom vanity is built with shaker-style white oak doors per a direct quote from our carpenter, with the stain/finish work completed as a separate step. In the former makeup nook, a pantry-style closet is built out with shelving. The vanity countertop is fabricated and installed, new baseboard goes in throughout, the three LED mirrors and your hardware (towel racks, TP holders) are installed, and the entire room is painted.
