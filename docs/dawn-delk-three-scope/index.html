Scope 01

Guest bathroom conversion.

$13,629

A standard tub-to-walk-in-shower conversion, including a frameless glass sliding door, full tile floor replacement, fresh paint throughout, and a toilet reset. The vanity and electrical are not part of this scope — neither is being modified.

Included

  • Remove existing tub & prep for conversion
  • Shower valve & drain — rough & finish plumbing
  • Shower curb/enclosure framing
  • Minor drywall repair
  • Full shower tile & waterproofing
  • Hot-mopped shower pan
  • Frameless glass sliding shower door — supplied & installed
  • New bathroom floor tile — install labor (~45.4 sq ft)
  • Full bathroom paint
  • Toilet reset

You Supply

  • All tile — shower walls, shower floor & bathroom floor
  • Shower valve/head/trim
  • Toilet, if replacing (reset of existing is included)
  • Any accessories
Not touched: the vanity and electrical are explicitly outside this scope — neither is being modified. Shower dimensions weren't precisely measured and are sized to a typical guest-bath footprint; final tile quantities may adjust once confirmed on-site.
Scope 02

Downstairs flooring.

$4,525.17

Removal of existing flooring and installation of new LVP flooring with a vapor barrier across three connected downstairs areas (kitchen and living room, ~491.9 sq ft total), plus new baseboard throughout.

Included

  • Remove existing flooring — all three areas (~491.9 sq ft)
  • Install new LVP flooring with vapor barrier — install labor
  • New baseboard throughout the flooring areas — labor & material

You Supply

  • LVP flooring material
Worth knowing: baseboard footage (~151.3 LF) is a full-perimeter estimate and doesn't account for doorways or openings between the kitchen and living room — actual footage may be lower if those areas are more open-concept. Demo pricing assumes standard flooring removal; we'll want to confirm the existing material type (tile, carpet, etc.) before starting.
Scope 03

Kitchen countertops.

$6,080.19

Fabrication and installation of new kitchen countertops with a 6" backsplash across three sections (~54.9 sq ft total), plus reconnecting the sink, garbage disposal, and dishwasher. This is fabrication and installation labor only — you select and pay for the stone material directly.

Included

  • Countertop fabrication & install — 3 sections (~54.9 sq ft)
  • 6" backsplash fabrication & install (~12.2 sq ft)
  • Reset the sink
  • Rough & finish plumbing — sink & garbage disposal
  • Connect the dishwasher

You Supply

  • Stone/slab material
  • Sink, if replacing (reset of existing is included)
  • Faucet
  • Garbage disposal unit
  • Dishwasher
Worth knowing: this price doesn't include the stone itself — you select and pay for that material separately. Final labor cost may vary depending on the stone type you choose, since some materials are more labor-intensive to fabricate than others.
