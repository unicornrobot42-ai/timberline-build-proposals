Project Overview

Two Full Bathroom Remodels

This proposal covers a complete remodel of both the 2nd-floor guest bathroom and the master bathroom in your San Clemente condo. All labor and construction materials are included in the pricing below. You supply all finish materials and fixtures — tile, flooring, vanities, countertop slabs, toilets, tub, shower valves and trim, mirrors, and accessories. We install everything you provide.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear most on projects like this.

Why is the guest bath more involved than a typical bathroom remodel?
Because there's no existing shower or tub in that bathroom today — this isn't a like-for-like swap, it's genuinely new construction. We're running all-new wet-wall plumbing, including a brand-new drain, to add a shower/tub where none exists. That's the single biggest driver of the guest-bath price.
What happens if you find something unexpected during demo?
Given the 2nd-floor location and the layout changes in both bathrooms, some conditions behind the walls and floors can't be fully known until demo happens. If we find something — water damage, outdated plumbing, structural surprises — we stop, show you what we found, and get your written approval before any additional work or cost is added. We recommend budgeting a contingency on top of this proposal for that reason.
Why do I supply my own tile, fixtures, and vanities?
This keeps your costs transparent and gives you full control over what goes into your home. You pick exactly what you want and pay exactly what it costs — no markup from us. Our price covers all the labor to install everything correctly.
Can I add the wall-mount toilet or microcement floor later?
Yes — these are both listed as optional upgrades and can be added any time before we reach that phase of construction. Just let us know and we'll confirm final pricing and adjust the schedule accordingly.
What's your warranty on the work?
All labor is warrantied for 1 year from project completion. Materials and fixtures carry their manufacturer warranties on top of that.
