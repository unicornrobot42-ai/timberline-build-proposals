The Project

From open studs to rent-ready.

A roof leak damaged the living room and kitchen ceilings, walls and flooring (Insurance Water Damage Report dated 10/22/2026). Demo and drying are complete. The space is open to studs and joists, insulation has been removed, the floor is bare slab, and the cabinets and countertops are out.

Timberline rebuilds both rooms to a builder-grade, rental-standard finish. We assume all appliances are on site and will be reused and reset.
