Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear most on projects like this.

Why does the shower/toilet relocation involve breaking foundation?
Moving a shower or toilet more than a few inches usually means relocating the drain and supply lines underneath the slab. That requires breaking the concrete foundation, trenching for the new plumbing path, and patching it back once the lines are moved — up to 5 feet in this case for both bathrooms. It's one of the bigger structural pieces of this project.
Can we use one bathroom while the other is under construction?
Yes — we can sequence the work so one bathroom stays usable while we're working in the other, which is one of the advantages of doing both together rather than fully gutting the whole home's bathroom access at once.
What happens if you find hidden damage during demo?
Any hidden damage discovered during demo is billed as a change order — we'll show you what we found and get your written approval before any additional work or cost is added.
Why do I supply my own tile, vanity tops, and fixtures?
This keeps your costs transparent and gives you full control over what goes into your home. You pick exactly what you want and pay exactly what it costs — no markup from us. Our price covers all the labor to install everything correctly.
What's your warranty on the work?
All labor is warrantied for 1 year from project completion. Manufacturer warranties apply to all materials we supply — glass, vent fans, and heated floor system if selected.
