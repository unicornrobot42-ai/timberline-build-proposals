A Note on Buying Extra

It's standard practice to buy 10–15% more tile than the exact square footage to account for cuts, waste, and pattern matching — especially here, since the shower has three separate wall panels plus a floor at a different plane. This is guidance to help you shop, not a hard requirement — the actual amount of overage really depends on the tile size and pattern you choose.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear most on projects like this.

Why do I need to buy extra tile?
Tile always has some waste — cuts around edges, corners, and fixtures, plus extra for pattern matching if your tile has any veining or directionality. With three separate shower wall panels plus a floor at a different plane, a bit of buffer keeps you from running short mid-install and having to special-order more (which can delay the job if it's out of stock). 10–15% over the exact square footage is a reasonable target, but it varies by tile size and layout.
Why do I supply my own tile, vanity, and fixtures?
This keeps your costs transparent and gives you full control over what goes into your home. You pick exactly what you want and pay exactly what it costs — no markup from us. Our price covers all the labor to install everything correctly.
What's your warranty on the work?
All labor is warrantied for 1 year from project completion. Materials and fixtures carry their manufacturer warranties on top of that.
