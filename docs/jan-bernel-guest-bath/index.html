Scope of Work

Demo through finish.

$14,200

The existing tub is removed and the space prepped for conversion. All plumbing needed for the shower and vanity — valve, drain, and rough/finish work — is completed as one consolidated scope. Framing is added for the shower curb/enclosure, with minor drywall repair throughout. The shower walls and floor are fully tiled and waterproofed, with a hot-mopped pan and a frameless glass door supplied and installed. New tile flooring goes in throughout the bathroom. The existing vent fan is replaced, outlets are updated (GFCI/outlet work), and the existing light fixture is swapped for your supplied fixture. A single-sink vanity up to 34" wide is set — installation only — and your mirror is hung. Minor touch-up painting finishes the space.

A Note on Buying Extra

It's standard practice to buy 15% more tile than the exact square footage to account for cuts, waste, and pattern matching — especially here, since the shower has three separate wall panels plus a floor at a different plane. This is guidance to help you shop, not a hard requirement — the actual amount of overage really depends on the tile size and pattern you choose.
