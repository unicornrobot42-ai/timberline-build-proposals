The Project

A modern refresh, same footprint.

We remove the maple-glaze raised-panel cabinets, the granite countertops and full-height granite backsplash, and the travertine tile floor with its gold diamond inserts. In their place go new pre-fab cabinets, new countertops, a new tile backsplash and new LVP flooring, in the style of the reference you shared.

The sink stays under the garden window, the recessed ceiling lights stay, and the layout doesn't change. Timberline provides labor and construction materials. Finish materials, appliances and fixtures are supplied by you, and we install everything you supply.

Scope of Work

Full kitchen, start to finish.

$36,679

The kitchen is a peninsula layout open to the bay-window dining nook, with a double sink under a garden window, a gas cooktop with hood, a double wall oven, and the fridge in a cased opening. All of that stays where it is. We demo down to the slab, build the new kitchen back up, and set your appliances.

Included

  • Protect work area and adjacent rooms — plastic sheeting and dust barriers
  • Disconnect and remove sink, faucet, disposal, dishwasher, cooktop, hood and wall oven
  • Demo all upper and base cabinets, including the tall oven unit and flanking pantry cabinets
  • Demo granite countertops and full-height granite backsplash
  • Demo travertine tile floor, including the mortar bed
  • Haul away all debris
  • Grind slab smooth after travertine removal
  • 6 mil vapor barrier over the prepared slab
  • Install LVP flooring in the kitchen (~140 sq ft)
  • New baseboard and trim
  • Reset sink, faucet, disposal and dishwasher
  • Reconnect gas line to the cooktop
  • Reconnect oven, cooktop, hood, dishwasher and disposal electrically
  • Set outlets and switches at the new cabinets and backsplash
  • Install under-cabinet lighting
  • Drywall patch and repair where the backsplash, cabinets and ceiling transitions were disturbed; prep walls for tile and paint
  • Pre-fab cabinets — base, wall, tall units, fridge surround, trim and fillers (allowance — see below)
  • Cabinet installation — base, wall and tall units, with crown and fillers
  • Install your pulls and knobs
  • Countertop template, fabrication and install (~50 sq ft, including the peninsula)
  • Cutouts for sink and cooktop; mount your undermount sink
  • Tile backsplash install (~35 sq ft), including setting materials and grout
  • Set cooktop, hood, double wall oven, dishwasher and refrigerator
  • Hook up fridge water line to the existing line
  • Paint walls, touch up ceiling, paint cabinet crown and trim

About the Cabinet Allowance

The $11,500 for pre-fab cabinets is an allowance, carried at our cost with no markup. You choose the style and finish, and the final amount is adjusted to the actual supplier invoice, up or down. Installation is priced separately and is included in the total.

Not Included

  • Relocating or extending the fridge water line. We hook up to the existing one only.
  • Gas line, hood duct or electrical changes beyond reconnecting the existing items.
  • Any change to the kitchen layout, such as moving walls, the sink, the cooktop or the peninsula.
  • Floor transitions. None are expected, so none are included.
  • Stone, tile, LVP, hardware and appliances, as listed under You Supply.

Good to Know

  • Quantities are estimated from photos (cabinets ~24 LF base, ~18 LF wall and 3 tall units; ~50 sq ft countertop; ~35 sq ft backsplash; ~140 sq ft flooring). We confirm everything by field measurement before ordering.
  • Slab condition: the grind-down price assumes a normal slab. Anything unforeseen in the slab or subfloor once the travertine comes up is handled by change order.
  • Your selections: cabinet style and finish, countertop stone, LVP and backsplash tile are your choices. The reference you shared shows the general look.
  • Changes in scope are handled by written change order.
