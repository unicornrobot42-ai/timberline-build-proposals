Project Overview

Opening Up the Space, Top to Bottom

This proposal covers a full remodel of your bathroom at 7 White Water Dr — starting with removing the wall and door that currently separate the toilet/shower area from the vanity, combining both into a single open room. From there, we're building an all-new 36"×60" walk-in shower, adding a new retrofit window, converting the vanity to a double-sink layout, running new tile flooring, and finishing with new doors, paint, and trim throughout.

All labor and construction materials are included in the price below. You supply all finish materials and fixtures — tile, flooring, vanity, countertop, sinks, faucets, toilet, door slabs, and shower trim — and we install everything.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear most on projects like this.

Why are you removing the wall between the two areas?
Combining the toilet/shower area with the vanity area into a single open room opens up the whole bathroom, improves flow, and gives the space a more elevated, modern feel. It's one of the bigger structural changes in this project and sets the layout for everything else — the new shower, the double vanity, and the retrofit window all work together in the new open plan.
What happens if you find something unexpected during demo?
Removing a wall and reworking plumbing can occasionally reveal conditions we couldn't see beforehand — water damage, outdated wiring, or similar. If that happens, we stop, show you what we found, and get your written approval before any additional work or cost is added.
Why do I supply my own tile, vanity, and fixtures?
This keeps your costs transparent and gives you full control over what goes into your home. You pick exactly what you want and pay exactly what it costs — no markup from us. Our price covers all the labor to install everything correctly.
Can I upgrade to a semi-custom vanity later?
Yes — just let us know before we reach that phase of construction and we'll get you pricing for the upgrade in place of the included pre-fabricated vanity install.
What's your warranty on the work?
All labor is warrantied for 1 year from project completion. Materials and fixtures carry their manufacturer warranties on top of that.
