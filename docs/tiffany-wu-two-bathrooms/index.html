Project Overview

Two Independent Bathroom Remodels

This proposal covers two separate bathroom remodels at 1990 Port Edward Circle — your son's bathroom and your daughter's bathroom. These are being done one at a time, not simultaneously, so each is priced and scoped as its own independent project. We're presenting both together here for planning purposes, but each has its own scope of work, its own price, and its own timeline.

All labor and construction materials are included in the pricing below. You supply all finish materials — tile, faucets, mirrors, hardware — and we install everything. The one exception on this project: both custom white oak vanities are being built and sourced by Timberline, not purchased separately by you. That's a departure from how we usually handle cabinetry, and it's called out clearly below so there's no confusion.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear most on projects like this.

Why are the vanities included this time, when your other proposals say I supply the cabinet?
On most of our projects, cabinetry is something you purchase and we install. On this project specifically, we're building both custom white oak vanities ourselves — so the material cost is part of our price, not something you need to shop for or buy separately. It's the one exception to our usual approach here.
Why is there no discount for doing both bathrooms?
Since these are sequential projects — one bathroom at a time rather than both simultaneously — each is priced as its own standalone job. If your plans change and you'd like to do both back-to-back or concurrently, let us know and we can talk through options.
What happens with the recessed shelving in the son's bathroom?
We won't know what's actually behind that wall until demo happens. Once it's open, we'll assess the cavity and get you accurate pricing for the shelving. If it turns out not to be feasible or cost-effective, we'll let you know that too before any work proceeds.
What about the stair rail?
Still being worked out — we need about a week to put together accurate pricing. Not included in either bathroom's total above.
What's included in the tankless water heater conversion?
Removing your existing tank water heater and installing a Noritz EZ 40 tankless unit in its place, for $3,800 total — all materials and labor included. This is a whole-house item, separate from either bathroom, and only gets added if you'd like to move forward with it.
What's your warranty on the work?
All labor is warrantied for 1 year from project completion. Materials and fixtures carry their manufacturer warranties on top of that.
