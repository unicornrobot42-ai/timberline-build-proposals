Project Overview

Two Full Gut Remodels

This proposal covers a full gut remodel of both bathrooms at 27961 Atherton. The guest bathroom is centered on a tub/shower combo with a refreshed vanity and finishes. The master bathroom is a more extensive reconfiguration — converting the built-in tub to a freestanding tub (relocating supply and drain), building a standalone 41×46×90 shower, and installing a large 140" double-sink vanity with an integrated makeup counter and heated vanity drawers.

All labor and construction materials are included in the price below. You supply all finish materials and fixtures — tile, tubs, cabinets, countertops, flooring, faucets, and toilets — and we install everything.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear most on projects like this.

What does the freestanding tub conversion actually involve?
Right now the master bath has a built-in tub. A freestanding tub sits on the floor rather than being built into a surround, which means the water supply lines need to be relocated and the drain moved to a floor-mounted configuration. The new tub goes roughly in the same area as the existing one, but it's not a like-for-like swap — there's real plumbing work involved, which is why we'd recommend a bit of contingency here specifically.
Why does the master have a separate shower and tub, but the guest bath doesn't?
The master bathroom is being built out with a standalone 41×46×90 shower that's fully separate from the freestanding tub. The guest bathroom keeps a more space-efficient tub/shower combo — a 36×60 shower configured over the tub rather than two separate fixtures.
What are the heated vanity drawers?
We're running the necessary power and installing the required hardware for two heated drawers in the master vanity — these are specialty drawer units (often used for warming towels or similar) that you'd purchase separately. Our scope covers making sure they're wired and ready to install; the units themselves are customer-supplied.
What happens if you find something unexpected during demo?
This is especially relevant around the freestanding tub plumbing relocation, where we can't fully see what's happening behind the walls or under the floor until demo is done. If we find something — old plumbing, water damage, or similar — we stop, show you what we found, and get your written approval before any additional work or cost is added.
Why do I supply my own tile, vanities, and fixtures?
This keeps your costs transparent and gives you full control over what goes into your home. You pick exactly what you want and pay exactly what it costs — no markup from us. Our price covers all the labor to install everything correctly.
What's your warranty on the work?
All labor is warrantied for 1 year from project completion. Materials and fixtures carry their manufacturer warranties on top of that.
