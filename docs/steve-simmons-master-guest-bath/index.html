Bathroom 1

Master Bath — Full Remodel

$24,815

A full remodel with a real layout change: the existing toilet room is being converted into a curbless walk-in shower behind a new pony wall, and the toilet relocates elsewhere in the master bathroom. The shower measures 60"×36" with a 96" ceiling, tiled floor to ceiling. A new 60" double-sink vanity with a quartz countertop is installed, along with new flooring, recessed lighting, and paint throughout.

Included

  • Site prep (demo already complete)
  • Remove toilet room wall; frame new pony wall
  • Drywall repair/patch throughout
  • Plumbing — relocate shower valve & drain, relocate toilet, double-vanity rough & finish
  • Curbless shower — walls, pony wall & floor tiled to ceiling, full waterproofing
  • Linear drain & sloped substrate for curbless shower pan
  • New tile flooring — install, full bathroom
  • 60" double-sink vanity — install only
  • Quartz countertop — fabrication & install
  • Recessed lighting (~4–5 fixtures)
  • Outlet/GFCI updates
  • Full bathroom paint, baseboard & trim

You Supply

  • All tile — shower & floor
  • Vanity cabinet
  • Quartz countertop material
  • Both sinks & faucets
  • Toilet (Timberline resets it in the new location)
  • Shower valve/head/trim
  • Light fixtures (Timberline installs the recessed housings)
  • Permits & fees, if required
Curbless upcharge: a $2,000 upcharge applies specifically for the curbless shower conversion — it's already reflected in the $24,815 price above. Curbless showers require a linear drain and sloped substrate, which takes more precise waterproofing than a standard shower pan.

Still Being Confirmed

  • Shower tile square footage is currently an estimate (~100 sq ft) pending confirmation of the exact wall count and pony wall height.
  • Overall bathroom floor square footage hasn't been provided yet — final flooring cost may vary based on the tile/material you choose.
  • No glass panel is included above the pony wall — this design assumes a fully open curbless shower. If you'd like a glass panel added, that would be an addition to scope.
