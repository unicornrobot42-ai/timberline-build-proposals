The Project

Three scopes, three different jobs.

The master bath is a finish and layout refinement — the toilet and shower both stay in their current locations, but the space becomes a true wet room with full-height tile, a curbless shower, and a relocated entry door. The guest bath is a straightforward tub-to-shower conversion. The HVAC scope is a full system replacement — new condenser, new furnace, and all-new ducting.

Timberline provides labor and construction materials. Finish materials — tile, vanities, countertops, sinks, faucets, fixtures — are supplied by you unless a scope specifically states otherwise.

Scope 1

Master Bath — Full Remodel

$29,120

This is a full remodel of the master bathroom. The toilet/shower area specifically is being converted into a true wet room — full-height tile to the 96" ceiling and a curbless shower — while the toilet and shower themselves stay in their current locations, so this is a finish and layout refinement there rather than a full fixture relocation. The entry door is being reframed to sit closer to the toilet area. Beyond the wet room itself, the rest of the bathroom is refreshed too: a new double-sink vanity with a fabricated countertop and backsplash, new lighting, closet doors, and fresh paint and trim throughout.

Included

  • Remaining prep & cleanup (bulk demo already complete)
  • Reframe entry door closer to the toilet area
  • Bump out wet room wall, flush with existing wall line
  • Drywall over former medicine cabinet opening
  • General drywall repair/patch, incl. new door opening
  • Texture ceiling
  • Plumbing — relocate shower valve/handle near door entrance, extend lines to existing shower head location
  • Double-sink vanity rough & finish plumbing
  • Curbless shower drain
  • Curbless shower conversion (upcharge included)
  • Full-height tile to ceiling — walls & floor (~244 sq ft), full waterproofing
  • Hot-mopped shower pan (curbless still requires a sloped mud pan)
  • Double-sink vanity — install only
  • Countertop fabrication & install, incl. 4" or 6" backsplash
  • Closet doors — install only
  • 2 new wet room lights — rough-in & install
  • Vanity light fixture — install
  • Up to 2 mirrors, 2 towel racks, 1 TP holder — install
  • Vent fan replacement
  • Ceiling paint
  • Baseboard & casing where needed
