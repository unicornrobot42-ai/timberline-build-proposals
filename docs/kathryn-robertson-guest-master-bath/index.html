The Project

Two bathrooms, calibrated for resale.

This is a pre-listing renovation — both bathrooms are scoped and priced to be cost-conscious throughout, since the goal is getting the home ready for sale rather than a full custom remodel. The guest bath converts the existing tub to a walk-in shower. The master bath keeps its existing tub and shower footprint entirely — the tub itself isn't touched, only the tile around it and in the shower is replaced.

Timberline provides labor and construction materials. Finish materials — tile, vanities, countertops, fixtures — are supplied by you.

  • Shower curtain rod — supplied & installed

Bathroom 2

Master Bath — Re-Tile, Existing Footprint

$16,940

The existing built-in tub stays exactly where it is — only the tiled border/apron around it is replaced. The shower keeps its current footprint and size, with all-new tile and frameless glass replacing the dated small-format tile currently in place.

Included

  • Remove old shower tile & tub border tile — surface-level demo only, tub itself is not removed or reset
  • Plumbing — new valve, drain & finish plumbing for shower and faucets
  • Re-tile the tub border/apron
  • Re-tile shower walls & floor, existing footprint
  • Hot-mopped shower pan
  • Frameless shower glass — supplied & installed, replacing existing curtain/swing door
  • Semi-custom double-sink vanity — install only
  • Countertop fabrication & install
  • Hang mirror, towel racks & TP holder
  • Paint & baseboard

You Supply

  • Vanity cabinet (semi-custom)
  • Countertop slab/material
  • All tile (tub border & shower)
  • Faucet & shower trim
  • Mirror, towel racks & TP holder
  • Permits & fees, if required

Still Being Confirmed

  • Tile square footage is estimated, not measured. Both the tub border re-tile (~18 sq ft est.) and the shower re-tile (~90 sq ft est.) are based on photos of the space rather than an on-site measurement — these are the two figures most likely to shift once confirmed.
  • Shower glass: this bathroom is priced with frameless glass. The guest bath is using a curtain instead to keep costs down — worth confirming whether the same swap makes sense here too, given the pre-listing cost goal.
  • Bathroom floor: this scope covers the shower floor and the tub border only. The rest of the bathroom floor outside the shower is not being replaced or refinished as part of this proposal.
