The Project

A refinish built around custom millwork.

This proposal covers a guest bathroom refresh centered on a custom boxed wainscot — a raised, recessed-panel millwork detail — built and installed on every wall, with wallpaper on the band above it. The vanity relocates, plumbing follows it, and a full paint and fixture refresh finishes the space.

Timberline provides labor and construction materials. Finish materials — the vanity cabinet, countertop slab, wallpaper, mirror, and hardware — are supplied by you.

Scope of Work

Millwork, plumbing & finish.

$9,590

The existing vanity is removed, and the supply and drain lines are relocated to center on the new vanity's location. A custom boxed wainscot — a raised, recessed-panel millwork detail — is built and installed on every wall of the bathroom, with wallpaper installed on the band of wall above it. A small floor patch (~3 sq ft) uses your own spare tile. The new vanity is set with a fabricated countertop, the ceiling and wainscot trim are painted, and your mirror, towel rack, and toilet paper holder are hung.
