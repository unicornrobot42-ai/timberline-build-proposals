Wellness Room Conversion

The existing office is being fully converted into a wellness room featuring a sauna. This includes removing the built-in desk and bookshelves, closing up an existing window opening, running dedicated power for the sauna and a fireplace-area TV, installing the sauna itself, and finishing both rooms with new crown molding, baseboard, and paint throughout.

  • Dedicated electrical power for a TV mounted over the fireplace
