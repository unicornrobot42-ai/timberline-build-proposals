Scope 02

Boys bathroom, full gut remodel.

$17,010

A complete gut remodel of the existing walk-in shower bathroom, plus a new single-sink vanity setup and new tile flooring. Shower measures 36×45 with ~100" ceilings. Fixtures stay in their current locations — all supply, drain, and valve work is being replaced new.

A completed walk-in shower from a recent Timberline project — for reference on finish quality, not a rendering of this specific bathroom.

Included

  • Full gut demolition
  • All new plumbing (fixtures stay in place)
  • Toilet reset
  • Framing & drywall repair as needed
  • Shower tile labor + full waterproofing
  • Hot-mopped shower pan
  • Glass shower door — supplied & installed
  • New tile flooring install (~74"×33")
  • GFCI outlets & lighting updates
  • Vanity install (labor only)
  • Countertop fabrication & install
  • Full bathroom paint · baseboard & trim

You Supply

  • All tile — shower & floor
  • Vanity cabinet
  • Countertop slab / material
  • Sink, faucet, shower fixtures
  • Toilet (if replacing — reset of existing is included)

Scope 04

Girls bathroom — shower & tub only.

$11,130

A targeted remodel focused on the shower/tub area only. The existing vanity and flooring are staying — they are not being touched, removed, or replaced. The project centers on a new built-in-style tub with a bench area, tile to the 108" ceiling, and a frameless sliding glass door. Shower/tub area measures 30×63.

A completed tile surround from a recent Timberline project — for reference on finish quality, not a rendering of this specific bathroom.

Included

  • Removal of existing tub/shower surround
  • Shower/tub plumbing (valve, drain, finish)
  • Framing for built-in bench area
  • Set new tub
  • Tile surround to 108" ceiling + bench + waterproofing
  • Frameless sliding glass door — supplied & installed

You Supply

  • The new tub
  • All tile
Explicitly excluded: existing vanity & existing flooring stay untouched.
