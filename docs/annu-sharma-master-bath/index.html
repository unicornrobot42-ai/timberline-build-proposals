Scope of Work

Full gut, new finishes throughout.

$42,840

A full gut of the existing bathroom — tub, shower, tile, vanity, countertop, flooring, and toilet all come out. Minor framing is added around the tub/shower surround, and drywall is repaired throughout. All plumbing is completed as one consolidated scope: setting the jacuzzi tub, the shower valve and head, rough and finish plumbing for the double-sink vanity, and the toilet. A dedicated GFCI circuit is added for the jacuzzi's pump, since the existing tub isn't jetted and this is new electrical work. The jacuzzi tub is set and connected, with a tiled deck/surround built around it to match the design. The shower — designed as a shower for two with a bench and niche — is fully tiled with complete waterproofing, a hot-mopped pan, and a glass enclosure. New tile flooring goes in throughout (~130–150 sq ft). The large double-sink vanity with its dedicated makeup section is set, with a matching countertop fabricated and installed. Faucets, shower trim, and the tub filler are installed, along with mirrors, towel racks, and TP holders. The wall-mount toilet is installed complete with its in-wall carrier system. The bathroom is fully painted with new baseboard and trim.
